About Area IQ™

What this is. A private presentation tool that compares Cape Coral's signature areas across the things that actually change a buying decision — utilities & assessments, flood & insurance exposure, boating access, schools, market pricing, and lifestyle. Gold-dashed outlines mark gated communities and named enclaves; tap them for their own scorecards.

Precision overlays. The Flood zones (FEMA) overlay draws the actual high-risk flood polygons (AE/A in blue, VE in red — FEMA's own color convention) from FEMA's effective National Flood Hazard Layer, and every scorecard's flood chart is computed from those same maps. The Sailboat water overlay is still hand-drawn — sailboat access is canal-by-canal, one bridge changes everything — so always verify the exact canal before writing an offer. Individual homes can differ from the mapped flood zone (elevation certificates & letters of map amendment) — always confirm per property.

Why price ranges, not just one number. In Cape Coral the same street can hold a $350K off-water home and a $1M+ gulf-access home. Each area's scorecard shows its price mix by product type so one blended median never tells the story alone.

Data version 0.8. Flood-zone shares and the flood overlay are real — computed from FEMA's effective flood maps (NFHL, sampled July 2026). Area boundaries are approximate and drawn for discussion. Market figures, rents, HOA fees, drive times, elevations, evacuation zones, and school letter grades (recent FDOE grades) remain ballpark estimates until county, school-district, and MLS feeds land in the next phase. Lee County is open-enrollment ("school choice"), so school assignment is never guaranteed by address. Always verify utilities, flood zone, bridge clearances, assessments, and schools for a specific property before relying on them.

Fair housing. This tool presents objective, third-party-sourced characteristics of places, never of people. Buyers set their own priorities. Equal Housing Opportunity.
