Common questions

Everything you want to know before booking.

Is the BioScan a medical diagnosis?

No. The Qest4 is a biofeedback assessment — it measures your body's galvanic skin response to energetic signatures and identifies stress patterns. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and it is not a substitute for medical care from your primary provider. It's a complementary wellness tool that gives Dr. Kassie a clearer map of where your body is asking for support.

Does it hurt? Are there any needles?

No needles, no pain, no preparation. You sit comfortably and hold two brass handpieces. The device sends gentle low-frequency signals and reads your response. The entire scan takes about 20 minutes.

How is this different from regular bloodwork?

Bloodwork is excellent at detecting structural change after it has happened. The BioScan picks up energetic stress patterns before they become diagnosable conditions — patterns your bloodwork can miss entirely. We recommend you continue working with your primary provider on labs. The BioScan is what fills in the gap when labs come back "normal" but you still don't feel right.

Do I have to buy supplements?

No. Your protocol is given to you in writing — you're free to source any supplements you want, anywhere. We carry practitioner-grade lines in-clinic and offer Fullscript ordering for convenience, but you're never required to purchase through us.

Will I see results in 90 days?

Most patients in the 90-Day Reset see meaningful shifts by Day 30 in energy, digestion, and sleep, with the biggest changes between Day 30 and Day 90 as the protocol does its work. The Day 90 re-scan shows the change visually — we put your Day-1 and Day-90 scans side by side. Results vary by patient, severity, and consistency with the protocol.

Does insurance cover this?

No. The BioScan is a cash-pay wellness service. HSA/FSA cards are accepted at our discretion (check with your plan administrator first). Payment plans are available for the 90-Day Reset and Inner Circle membership.

Can I add this to my existing care at Vibrant?

Yes — and that's where it really shines. Pair the BioScan with chiropractic, Softwave therapy, HBOT, cryotherapy, footbath detox, and our nutrition team for a full root-cause approach. Inner Circle members receive discounts on all of these.

Honest assessment

What could break this model.

Practitioner bandwidth

Dr. Kassie already runs a busy chiro practice. Realistic = 7 scans/wk = ~9 hrs/wk. Aggressive needs 18+ hrs/wk which likely means training Alana or another delivery practitioner by month 4.

FDA / FTC claims risk

ZYTO has been warned twice; RoyalVibe got a warning letter in 2022. Stay clear of "detects / diagnoses / treats." Use "stress patterns / energetic information / supports the body." Signed intake form is the firewall.

Content engine flakiness

If nobody posts the scan-reveal Reels weekly, Realistic collapses to Conservative. Hire the content creator before the scanner arrives, not after.

Supplement dependency

~$36k of Realistic net comes from the supplement spine. If patients buy on Amazon instead, that line halves. The in-clinic dispensary + member-discount perk is what keeps spend at Vibrant.

Competitor wakes up

Quiet Waters (Bonita) and OMNI (Naples) could mirror this within 6 months. First-mover moat in Cape Coral is 25 miles — own SEO + IG share-of-voice before they notice.

Cape Coral patient mix

Seasonal — snowbird ramp Nov–Apr, dip May–Oct. Plan ad spend and Reset cohorts to lean into the season.
