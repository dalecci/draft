The doctors

Dr. Kassie

Founder · Chiropractic Physician

Founder of Vibrant Health and Wellness, Dr. Kassandra "Kassie" Francis, DC built this practice on a simple belief: people deserve a doctor who takes the time to understand them. She's passionate about pediatric and family care and known for tackling the tough cases.

More about Dr. Kassie

Dr. Kassie takes a holistic, whole-body approach — using an in-depth knowledge of the body's complex systems to connect the dots and identify the root cause of pain and dysfunction. Whether you're an athlete chasing performance, a new mom recovering after birth, or a parent looking for gentle care for your child, she and her team are here to help you thrive.

Dr. Carrie

Chiropractic Physician

With more than 26 years of clinical experience, Dr. Carrie is dedicated to helping patients achieve better health through personalized, patient-centered care — restoring function, relieving discomfort, and supporting the body's natural ability to heal.

More about Dr. Carrie

Her expertise spans a wide variety of chiropractic techniques, allowing her to tailor care to patients of all ages and a broad range of conditions. Every treatment plan is designed around each patient's unique needs — empowering them to move better, feel better, and reach their long-term health goals.

Dr. Vicki

Chiropractic Physician · Women's Health

Dr. Vicki Hemmett brings more than 24 years of experience helping women achieve healthier pregnancies and stronger pelvic health — with compassionate, evidence-informed care through every stage of motherhood.

More about Dr. Vicki

A recognized leader in women's health, Dr. Hemmett has authored two books and developed a proprietary musculoskeletal approach to treating pelvic floor dysfunction. She was one of the first chiropractors in the United States to incorporate internal pelvic floor therapy into clinical practice — helping women reduce pain, improve function, and feel confident throughout pregnancy, postpartum, and beyond.

The specialists

Rachel

Massage Therapist · Lymphatic Specialist

Rachel Fors, LMT, CLT is a certified lymphatic specialist who helps patients restore balance, reduce swelling, and support the body's natural healing and detoxification processes.

More about Rachel

Trained through the Academy of Lymphatic Studies, Rachel combines evidence-based techniques with a personalized approach. Her experience treating lymphedema, lipedema, post-surgical recovery, digestive concerns, and chronic swelling lets her create customized plans that help patients move more comfortably and heal more efficiently.

Alana

Bioresonance Practitioner

Alana Dyer is a bioenergetic testing practitioner and holistic nutrition coach who takes a whole-person approach — considering the physical, emotional, environmental, and energetic factors behind your wellness.

More about Alana

Combining bioenergetic testing with training in holistic nutrition and systems biology, Alana builds customized care plans designed to uncover underlying imbalances rather than simply chasing symptoms — helping patients understand their bodies and support lasting wellness.

Ashley

Massage Therapist

Ashley Roque, LMT believes massage should do more than provide temporary relief. With over nine years of experience, she builds individualized plans that address the root causes of discomfort.

More about Ashley

Her extensive background working alongside chiropractors has given her expertise in treating acute and chronic pain, muscle dysfunction, injury recovery, and preventative care — helping clients reduce pain, relieve stress, improve movement, and understand their bodies better.
