Common questions

Frequently asked.

Does Softwave therapy hurt?

No. Most patients describe it as a mild pulsing or tapping sensation. There may be brief tenderness during treatment in particularly inflamed areas, but the session itself is comfortable and well-tolerated.

How many sessions will I need?

Many patients feel meaningful relief after just one session. For chronic or deep-rooted conditions, we typically recommend a series of 4–6 sessions, spaced about a week apart, to maximize long-term tissue healing.

How long is each session?

Treatment itself usually takes about 10–15 minutes. Including check-in and discussion, plan on 20–30 minutes for your visit.

Is there any downtime or recovery?

None. You can return to normal activities — including exercise — immediately after your session. Some patients notice mild soreness for a day or two as the healing process kicks in, similar to a workout.

Is Softwave covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. Many insurers don't yet cover shockwave therapy, but we offer affordable package options to make care accessible. Give us a call and we'll walk you through your options.

Who shouldn't get Softwave therapy?

Softwave is safe for most adults, but it's not appropriate during pregnancy, over active cancers, or for patients with certain blood disorders or pacemakers. We'll review your full history at your first visit to make sure it's right for you.
