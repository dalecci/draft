03 / The boys gap

Quebec's boys-vs-girls grad gap — closed.

Quebec's official seven-year secondary graduation rate is 82.4% overall (2017-18 cohort, reported 2023-24). The gender breakdown matters: girls at 86.9%, boys at 78.2% — a gap of nearly nine points. Sport-Études programs report graduation rates that close this gap or erase it entirely.

04 / The pipeline

The athletic outcome is real, too.

  • Canada exported 168 players to NCAA Division I men's basketball for 2025–26 — the largest foreign-country pipeline. Quebec Sport-Études alumni feature prominently.
  • Basketball Québec operates Sport-Études basketball across 19 regions of the province.
  • Alumni include Olympic gold medalists (Alexandre Bilodeau — twice), Olympic alpine and freestyle skiers, and multiple national-team basketball players.
