02 / What we're building

A serious basketball pathway, inside a serious school.

01 / ACADEMICS

Academics first. Always.

Enrollment is conditional on academic performance. Slip in the classroom, lose training hours. Refined over 50 years, the results speak: Sport-Études students outperform the general Quebec population on every academic measure.

02 / COACHING

Real coaching. Real basketball.

15+ hours per week of federation-certified training inside the academic day. Not after-school practice. Not a glorified gym class. Professional-grade development for student-athletes who want a serious pathway.

03 / TRACK RECORD

A model that works.

Quebec's framework spans 66 sport disciplines, 600+ programs, and produces athletes who reach NCAA D1, the NHL, the WNBA, and the Olympics — while graduating at higher rates than the general population.

03 / The proof

The data isn't hopeful.
It's settled.

Across every measured academic outcome — HS graduation, CEGEP first-session success, college persistence — Sport-Études students sit at or above the general Quebec population. Often by a wide margin.
