00 / What this is

A half-day school. A half-day basketball program. For your student-athlete.

AM / SCHOOL

Half day school.

An efficient, accelerated academic curriculum. Full secondary requirements covered in one focused block per day.

PM / TRAINING

Half day sports.

15+ hours per week of elite basketball training. On-court skill, off-court strength, basketball IQ, and the character work most programs skip. All inside the school day, not after it.

FOR / THE ATHLETE

Built for the kid who refuses to choose.

For student-athletes who want elite training and a real academic foundation. Continued enrollment is conditional on academic performance.

01 / What your kid becomes here

Three pillars. One whole kid. Not a basketball factory.

A program that takes the basketball seriously because it takes the young person seriously. Skill, strength, intelligence, and character, built together, on purpose, every day.

01 / THE ATHLETE

A complete athlete. Not just a player.

Basketball fundamentals drilled daily. Shooting, footwork, ball-handling, defense. Basketball IQ built through film and live decision-making. Sport-specific strength and conditioning. Nutrition, sleep, mobility, recovery, treated as part of the work and not as extras. AI-powered tools (computer-vision shot analysis, AI-tagged film, training plans built from your kid's own data) used to accelerate the development.

02 / THE STUDENT

A real education. Every door still open.

An accelerated secondary curriculum delivered with focus. Mandatory tutoring and supervised study hall built into every day. NCAA academic eligibility preserved from day one. Reading, writing, and study habits that travel anywhere. The kid who graduates from here has the basketball door open, and every other door too.

03 / THE LEADER

The part most basketball programs skip.

Show up on time. Own your mistakes. Mentor the younger player. Handle losing without quitting. Handle winning without strutting. Speak clearly when it matters. Discipline, accountability, integrity, all built through daily practice and modeled by coaches who have actually done the thing. The kind of character that lasts long past the final buzzer.

The basketball outcomes are the byproduct. The young person is the point.

03 / What we're building

A serious basketball pathway, inside a serious school.

01 / ACADEMICS

Academics first. Always.

Enrollment is conditional on academic performance. Slip in the classroom, lose training hours. Refined over 50 years, the results speak: Sport-Études students outperform the general Quebec population on every academic measure.

02 / COACHING

Real coaching. Real basketball.

15+ hours per week of elite training inside the academic day. Not after-school practice. Not a glorified gym class. Professional-grade development for student-athletes who want a serious pathway.

03 / TRACK RECORD

A model that works.

Quebec's framework spans 66 sport disciplines and 600+ programs. It produces athletes who reach NCAA D1, the NHL, the WNBA, and the Olympics, while graduating at higher rates than the general population.
