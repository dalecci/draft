The Program

Half day school. Half day basketball. For your student-athlete.

An efficient, accelerated academic curriculum in one daily block. 15+ hours per week of elite basketball training in the other. The training covers on-court skill, off-court strength, basketball IQ, and the leadership and discipline most programs skip. Block order rotates day to day. Mandatory tutoring. Conditional enrollment. A structure refined in Quebec since the 1970s.

01 / What it is

A school-sanctioned dual pathway. Not a basketball academy.

Sport-Études is a school-sanctioned educational track originally formalized by the Quebec Ministère de l'Éducation in partnership with provincial sport federations and accredited clubs. The model has existed in France since 1974 and in its current Quebec form for over three decades.

The principle is simple. Students complete the full secondary academic curriculum in one daily block. In a second block, they receive 15+ hours per week of structured, sport-specific training taught by experienced coaches inside the school day. The order of the two blocks rotates day to day. Continued enrollment is conditional on academic performance. The structure is non-negotiable, and the students know it from day one.

02 / A typical day

Two daily blocks. Books and basketball. The order rotates.

  • 30 MIN

    Arrival & breakfast

    The day starts together. A fueled body and a clear head, on time.

  • BLOCK A · 3.5 HRS

    Core academics

    Mathematics, language arts, sciences. Full secondary curriculum, delivered in one focused block per day.

  • 60 MIN

    Lunch & study hall

    Eat. Reset. Hit the day's reading.

  • BLOCK B · 3 HRS

    Elite basketball training

    Skill drills, strength & conditioning, film study, scrimmage. Plus a daily dose of the leadership and discipline work most programs skip. Three hours of professional-grade work, inside the school day.

  • 60 MIN

    Mandatory tutoring & academic recovery

    Supervised. Targeted. The day ends with whatever needs to be cleaned up academically, before anyone goes home.

Block A and Block B rotate day to day. Some days the academic block opens; other days the training block does. Either way, both happen, every day.

03 / What your kid becomes here

Three pillars. One whole kid. Not a basketball factory.

A program that takes the basketball seriously because it takes the young person seriously. Skill, strength, intelligence, and character, built together, on purpose, every day.

01 / THE ATHLETE

A complete athlete. Not just a player.

Basketball fundamentals drilled daily. Shooting, footwork, ball-handling, defense. Basketball IQ built through film and live decision-making. Sport-specific strength and conditioning. Nutrition, sleep, mobility, recovery, treated as part of the work and not as extras. AI-powered tools (computer-vision shot analysis, AI-tagged film, training plans from your kid's own data) used to accelerate the development.

02 / THE STUDENT

A real education. Every door still open.

An accelerated secondary curriculum delivered with focus. Mandatory tutoring and supervised study hall built into every day. NCAA academic eligibility preserved from day one. Reading, writing, and study habits that travel anywhere. The kid who graduates from here has the basketball door open, and every other door too.

03 / THE LEADER

The part most basketball programs skip.

Show up on time. Own your mistakes. Mentor the younger player. Handle losing without quitting. Handle winning without strutting. Speak clearly when it matters. Discipline, accountability, integrity, all built through daily practice and modeled by coaches who have actually done the thing. The kind of character that lasts long past the final buzzer.

The basketball outcomes are the byproduct. The young person is the point.

05 / Why basketball, why Florida

The infrastructure is here.
The discipline isn't.

Florida is already the dominant US state for basketball academy formation. IMG Academy and Montverde Academy together account for 11 first-round NBA Draft picks since 2021. The demand and infrastructure are here. What's missing is a program that combines that elite training environment with the academic rigor and structural discipline of the European model.

We are bringing the Quebec Sport-Études framework to Florida, focused on basketball, and built from day one for families who want both.
