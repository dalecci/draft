The Program

Half day school. Half day basketball. For your student-athlete.

An efficient, accelerated academic curriculum in the morning. 15+ hours per week of elite basketball training in the afternoon — built on three pillars: fundamentals, basketball IQ, and AI-powered player development. Mandatory tutoring. Conditional enrollment. A structure refined in Quebec since the 1970s.

01 / What it is

A school-sanctioned dual pathway. Not a basketball academy.

Sport-Études is a school-sanctioned educational track originally formalized by the Quebec Ministère de l'Éducation in partnership with provincial sport federations and accredited clubs. The model has existed in France since 1974 and in its current Quebec form for over three decades.

The principle is simple. Students complete the full secondary academic curriculum in the morning. In the afternoon, they receive 15+ hours per week of structured, sport-specific training taught by experienced coaches inside the school day. Continued enrollment is conditional on academic performance. The structure is non-negotiable, and the students know it from day one.

02 / A typical day

Mornings for the books. Afternoons for the game. Evenings for the recovery.

  • 7:30 – 8:00 AM

    Arrival, breakfast

    The day starts together. A fueled body and a clear head, on time.

  • 8:00 – 11:30 AM

    Core academics

    Mathematics, language arts, sciences. Full secondary curriculum, taught in the highest-energy hours of the day.

  • 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM

    Lunch and study hall

    Eat. Reset. Hit the day's reading before the gym.

  • 12:30 – 3:30 PM

    Elite basketball training

    Built on three pillars: fundamentals, basketball IQ, and AI-powered player development. Skill work, strength & conditioning, film, scrimmage. Three hours of professional-grade work, inside the school day.

  • 3:30 – 4:30 PM

    Mandatory tutoring & academic recovery

    Supervised. Targeted. The day ends with whatever needs to be cleaned up academically, before anyone goes home.

03 / The training, three ways

The afternoon is built on three pillars.

Not three buzzwords. Each one shows up in the daily schedule, in the film room, and in the weekly performance benchmarks.

01 / FUNDAMENTALS

The basics, drilled daily.

Shooting form. Footwork. Ball-handling. Passing. Defensive stance. The boring, repetitive work that separates good players from great ones. No skipping the reps.

02 / BASKETBALL IQ

Read the floor.

How to make the right read at the right speed. Film study, situational basketball, decision-making under pressure. The mental side of the game where elite players actually live.

03 / AI

Modern tools, applied here.

Computer-vision shot analysis. AI-tagged game film. Training plans auto-generated from a player's own data. The tools NCAA D1 and pro programs use — applied at the high-school level.

05 / Why basketball, why Florida

The infrastructure is here.
The discipline isn't.

Florida is already the dominant US state for basketball academy formation — IMG Academy and Montverde Academy together account for 11 first-round NBA Draft picks since 2021. The demand and infrastructure are here. What's missing is a program that combines that elite training environment with the academic rigor and structural discipline of the European model.

We are bringing the Quebec Sport-Études framework to Florida, focused on basketball, and built from day one for families who want both.
