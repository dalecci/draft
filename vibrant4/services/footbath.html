Footbath
Detox.

Sometimes the simplest approaches are the most refreshing. A footbath detox is a gentle, relaxing way to support your body's natural cleansing processes — helping you feel lighter, more energized, and ready to take on whatever comes next.

What Is Footbath Detox?

A footbath detox uses warm water and a gentle electrical current to help draw impurities from the body through the feet. It's a simple, non-invasive way to support your body's natural detox pathways.

What To Expect

You'll sit comfortably with your feet in warm water for the duration of the session — it's deeply relaxing. Many people use it as a reset, especially alongside other treatments in their wellness routine.

Who It Helps

Anyone looking to support their body's detox systems, boost their energy, or simply take a moment to recharge. It pairs wonderfully with other services at Vibrant Health & Wellness for a full-body reset.
