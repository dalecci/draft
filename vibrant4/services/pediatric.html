Pediatric &
Prenatal.

Children deserve to grow up thriving — not just symptom-free. Our pediatric chiropractic care supports brain development, sensory processing, and whole-body wellness from infancy through adolescence — while expectant mothers get gentle care for a more comfortable pregnancy.

What Is Pediatric & Prenatal?

Pediatric chiropractic uses gentle, age-appropriate techniques to support a child's developing spine and nervous system. Adjustments for little ones look very different from adult care — they're soft, precise, and safe from day one. For moms-to-be, pregnancy-safe techniques bring comfort through every trimester.

What To Expect

We take time to get to know your child and put both them and you at ease. Care is always gentle, and we'll explain every step so parents feel confident and informed throughout the process.

Who It Helps

Newborns, infants, toddlers, and older children — as well as expectant mothers looking for a more comfortable pregnancy. If you're wondering whether your child could benefit, we'd love to chat.
