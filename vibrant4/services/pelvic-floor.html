Pelvic Floor
Care.

Specialized assessment and rehabilitation for pelvic floor dysfunction, pregnancy-related discomfort, and postpartum recovery, led by Dr. Vicki Hemmett, one of the first chiropractors in the United States to bring internal pelvic floor therapy into clinical practice.

What Is Pelvic Floor Care?

Pelvic floor care looks at the relationship between breathing, posture, core function, and pelvic mechanics. Over more than 24 years in women's health, Dr. Vicki has developed a proprietary musculoskeletal approach to treating pelvic floor dysfunction and has authored two books on the subject.

What To Expect

A private, unhurried evaluation of posture, breathing, core and pelvic function, including both the internal and external structures of the pelvic floor, with findings explained clearly and a plan built around your comfort. Care always moves at your pace, and we coordinate with your other providers when it's helpful.

Who It Helps

Women navigating pregnancy-related discomfort, postpartum recovery, pelvic pain, or pelvic floor dysfunction at any stage of life.
