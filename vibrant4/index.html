Frequently Asked Questions.

What services do you offer?

Nine services under one roof: chiropractic, pediatric & prenatal chiropractic, Softwave therapy, bioresonance scanning, massage & lymphatic therapy, cranial sacral, cryotherapy, hyperbaric oxygen therapy, and footbath detox.

How do I get started?

The easiest way is to book online — it takes about two minutes. Prefer to talk to a human? Call us at (239) 789-6959 and we'll get you set up.

What should I expect on my first visit?

A conversation first, care second. We'll take time to understand your history and goals, do a thorough evaluation, and explain what we find in plain English — then build a plan together. No pressure, no mystery.

Can my infant receive chiropractic care?

Absolutely — pediatric care is one of our specialties. Adjustments for infants are extremely gentle (about the pressure you'd use to check a ripe tomato) and support healthy development from day one. Our team is wonderful with little ones.

What makes you different?

Most offices offer one kind of care. We bring chiropractors, massage and lymphatic therapists, and bioresonance specialists together on one plan — so instead of bouncing between offices, your whole care team is already talking to each other.

How can I contact you?

Call (239) 789-6959, email frontdesk@vibranthealthswfl.com, or stop by at 1216 SW 4th St, Suite 6, Cape Coral, FL 33991.
