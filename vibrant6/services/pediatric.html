Helping Little Ones Thrive
From The Very Beginning.

Every child develops in their own unique way. From the earliest days of life, we're passionate about understanding how the nervous system, movement, structural development, and sensory experiences work together.

What Is Pediatric & Prenatal?

Our approach goes beyond checking the spine. We take a detailed look at your child's history, movement patterns, muscle tone, cranial and spinal mechanics, and developmental milestones to better understand their individual needs. Adjustments for little ones are soft, precise, and age-appropriate, and moms-to-be get pregnancy-safe techniques for comfort through every trimester.

What To Expect

We take time to get to know your child and put both them and you at ease. Care is always gentle, and we'll explain every step so parents feel confident and informed. Whether you're navigating feeding challenges with a newborn, noticing a preference to turn one direction, or have questions about your child's development, we're here to listen, evaluate, educate, and help guide your next steps.

Who It Helps

Newborns, infants, toddlers, and older children, as well as expectant mothers looking for a more comfortable pregnancy. If you're wondering whether your child could benefit, we'd love to chat.

Our Evaluation

What We Look For.

Cranial & Structural Function

Head shape, cranial asymmetry, spinal mobility, and areas of tension.

Movement & Motor Development

Head control, symmetry, posture, muscle tone, and age-appropriate motor milestones.

Nervous System Development

Age-appropriate reflexes, coordination, and observable sensory and motor responses.

Feeding & Oral Function

Jaw and neck mobility, oral tension, and feeding-related movement concerns.

Newborn & Infant Care

Why Families
Bring Their Babies.

  • Feeding challenges or oral tension in a newborn
  • A strong preference to turn or tilt to one side
  • Questions about head shape or cranial asymmetry
  • Questions about milestones, movement, or muscle tone
  • A fussy little one who is hard to settle
