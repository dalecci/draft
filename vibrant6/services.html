Beyond The Menu

Functional Neurology & Development.

Not a separate appointment type: a lens we bring to every evaluation.

Our doctors use neurological screening and developmental assessment, observing reflexes, coordination, movement patterns, and sensory responses, to understand how your nervous system is functioning. It shapes which techniques we choose, how we track progress, and when we recommend another provider's perspective. It's central to how Dr. Kassie approaches complex cases and how we evaluate infants and children.
