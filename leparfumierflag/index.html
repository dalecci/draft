How this works

What FLAG does

Every few days, an automated scan walks through your inventory and checks each product for signs the brand is discontinuing it: brand site listing status, major retailer availability, and community chatter (forums, resale listings, social posts). Anything suspicious lands here on the Flagged page with the reason and the sources behind it.

Confirmed vs rumored

Rumored means early chatter, worth watching. Confirmed means multiple independent sources agree. Once confirmed, FLAG also checks what the item is reselling for elsewhere, so you can judge the premium before deciding whether to buy in.

Notifications

You get emailed once per new flag. Already flagged items will not keep resurfacing in your inbox, they just stay listed here.

Reviewing and ordering

Every flag has a Confirm and Reject button, that is your own read on it, separate from the AI's status. There is also an ordered quantity field for anything you decide to buy in. Sort and filter by either one to see where things stand.

Checking stock

Select one or more flagged items with the checkboxes, then Check stock for selected to upload a current supplier price list. It matches by barcode where possible and shows what that supplier currently has for each item you picked.

Data connection

This page always shows the current flag list, no setup needed. Connect a token in the gear icon for two extras: wholesale cost on Inventory, and saving your review checkmarks and order quantities so they show up on every device instead of just this browser.
