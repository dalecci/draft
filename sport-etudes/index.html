What this is

A half-day school. A half-day basketball program. For your student-athlete.

Half day school

An efficient, accelerated academic curriculum. The full secondary requirements, taught in the highest-energy hours of the day — mornings only.

Half day sports

15+ hours per week of elite basketball training, built on three pillars — fundamentals, basketball IQ, and AI-powered player development. All inside the school day, not after it.

For the student-athlete

For kids who refuse to choose between elite training and a real academic foundation. Continued enrollment is conditional on academic performance.

The afternoon, three ways

What the basketball half of the day actually consists of.

Three pillars. Not three buzzwords. Each one shows up in the daily schedule, in the film room, and in the weekly performance benchmarks.

1. Fundamentals

The non-negotiable basics. Shooting form. Footwork. Ball-handling. Passing. Defensive stance. The boring, repetitive work that separates good players from great ones. Every player, every day.

2. Basketball IQ

How to read the floor. How to make the right read at the right speed. Film study, situational basketball, decision-making under pressure. The mental side of the game where elite players actually live.

3. AI-powered development

Computer-vision shot analysis. AI-tagged game film. Training plans auto-generated from each player's own data. The modern tools NCAA D1 and pro programs use — applied here, at the high-school level.

What we're building

A serious basketball pathway, inside a serious school.

Academics first, always

Enrollment is conditional on academic performance. Slip in the classroom, lose training hours. The structure has been refined over 50 years and the results speak for themselves: Sport-Études students outperform the general Quebec student population on every academic measure we track.

Real coaching. Real basketball.

15+ hours per week of elite training inside the academic day. Not after-school practice. Not a glorified gym class. Professional-grade development for student-athletes who want a serious basketball pathway — and the academic foundation to support whatever they choose next.

A model with a track record

Quebec's Sport-Études framework spans 66 sport disciplines, 600+ programs, and produces athletes who reach NCAA Division I, the NHL, the WNBA, and the Olympics — while graduating at higher rates than the general population. We are bringing that model to Florida.

The proof, not the pitch

The data isn't hopeful. It's settled.

Every academic metric we measure — high school graduation, CEGEP first-session success, college persistence — shows Sport-Études students at or above the general Quebec population. Often by a wide margin.
