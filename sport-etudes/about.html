Our position

Florida has the basketball infrastructure. What it lacks is a serious dual-pathway school — one that takes the academics every bit as seriously as the game. That gap is what this program exists to fill.

We are not trying to out-train Montverde or out-recruit IMG. We are building something they're structurally unable to build: a school where the basketball is excellent because the academics are non-negotiable, not in spite of it.

What we're committed to from day one

  • Conditional enrollment on academic performance. Slip in the classroom, lose training hours. No exceptions.
  • Federation-certified coaching staff. Every coach has competed at college, professional, or international levels.
  • 15+ hours of training per week inside the school day. Not after school. Not weekends only. Built into the schedule.
  • Mental health, wellbeing, and training-load caps as core program functions. Annual third-party wellbeing audit.
  • Complete transparency on outcomes. Every number we publish will be traceable to a primary source.

The long horizon

This is a multi-decade build. The Quebec model took 50 years to produce the data we now cite. We are not in a rush. We are in it to do this properly — and to make this the program families across the southeastern US move toward rather than the one they tolerate for a few seasons.
