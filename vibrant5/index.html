Before You Book

Quick Answers.

What should I expect on my first visit?

A conversation first, care second. We'll take time to understand your history and goals, do a thorough evaluation, and explain what we find in plain English — then build a plan together. No pressure, no mystery.

Can my infant receive chiropractic care?

Absolutely — pediatric care is one of our specialties. Adjustments for infants are extremely gentle (about the pressure you'd use to check a ripe tomato) and support healthy development from day one.

What makes you different?

Most offices offer one kind of care. We bring chiropractors, massage and lymphatic therapists, and bioresonance specialists together on one plan — so your whole care team is already talking to each other.

How do I get started?
