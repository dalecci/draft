La Nostra Storia

The same kitchen, now run by the people who grew up in it.

Cibo opened on McGregor Boulevard on 30 July 2004 and has been a Fort Myers landmark ever since. Not a chain, not a group. One dining room, cooking Italian and Mediterranean food with local ingredients and making as much as possible on premises.

In September 2025 the restaurant changed hands, and barely changed at all. Chef Sean Deckter and his wife Carmen bought it from the previous owner, Craig Komatz. Both have worked here since 2005, Sean in the kitchen and Carmen out on the floor. The people who cook your dinner and greet you at the door are the same people who have done it for two decades. They simply own the place now.

Sean Executive Chef and Owner, since 2005

Writes the menu and the nightly specials, and runs the kitchen through dinner service.

Carmen Hospitality and Service, since 2005

Looks after the room, and after the staff who have made a career of looking after you.

Reservations and takeout

Both are taken by phone only. Online booking through Google, OpenTable and the rest is not currently accepted. If you call before 3:00pm, leave a voicemail with your name, date, time and party size, and the restaurant will call you back.

239.454.3700
Booking for five or more

Parties of five or more are held with a valid credit card. Nothing is charged unless the reservation is not honoured, or it is cancelled outside the window.

A fee of $25 per guest applies only if the party does not show, or does not cancel in time. Reservations must be cancelled or changed at least 4 to 24 hours ahead.

Large tables mean pushing several tables together and turning other guests away, so a no show costs the kitchen and the servers a whole evening. The card simply protects that.

Gift certificates
